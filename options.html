AI Tab Grouper

Privacy: to group your tabs, this extension sends the titles and addresses (without query strings) of the tabs in the current window, plus the names of your existing tab groups, to the AI provider you chose above, and to nobody else. It does not run in incognito windows. Your API key is stored only on this device. Click the toolbar icon (default shortcut Alt+Shift+G, change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts) to regroup every tab in the window from scratch, ignoring existing groups.
